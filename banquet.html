The Summit Grand Ballroom

Our magnificent Summit Grand Ballroom is the perfect venue for weddings, corporate events, gala dinners, and milestone celebrations. With a capacity of up to 500 guests, state-of-the-art AV systems, and a dedicated events team, we ensure every occasion is flawlessly executed.

Surrounded by breathtaking mountain views and adorned with elegant chandeliers, the ballroom creates an atmosphere of grandeur and sophistication that your guests will remember forever.
