Indulge in Every Comfort

From relaxation to socialising, Kites Mountain offers a complete experience for every guest.

Outdoor Swimming Pool

Enjoy our year-round outdoor swimming pool and open-air bath, set amid the hills of Badulla. A relaxing spot for guests of all ages, free for all guests to use throughout their stay.

  • Open all year
  • Open-air bath
  • Free for all guests
  • Outdoor furniture & loungers

Breakfast & Dining

Start your day with a full English or Irish breakfast served with fresh juice and fruit. Room service and a dedicated dining area are also available for relaxed meals throughout your stay.

  • Full English/Irish breakfast
  • Fresh juice & fruit included
  • Room service available
  • On-site dining area

Free Airport Shuttle

Travel with ease using our complimentary airport shuttle service. Our friendly drivers are always ready to help, making your arrival and departure smooth and stress-free.

  • Free pick-up & drop-off
  • Friendly, helpful drivers
  • Easy access to Badulla town
  • 24-hour front desk assistance

Free High-Speed WiFi

Stay connected throughout your stay with complimentary WiFi available in all areas of the property, free of charge. Perfect for keeping in touch or working remotely during your visit.

  • Available in all rooms
  • Free of charge
  • Available in all areas
  • No extra fees

Free Private Parking

Arrive with peace of mind — free private parking is available on-site, no reservation needed. The property also offers 24-hour security and CCTV coverage for added safety.

  • Free on-site parking
  • No reservation needed
  • 24-hour security & CCTV
  • Safety deposit box at front desk
