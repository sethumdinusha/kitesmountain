World-Class Facilities

Indulge in Every Comfort

From relaxation to socialising, Kites Mountain offers a complete experience for every guest.

Infinity Pool

Our stunning infinity pool appears to merge seamlessly with the mountain horizon. Open year-round with heated water, poolside loungers, and a dedicated pool attendant to ensure your comfort.

  • Open 6 AM – 10 PM daily
  • Heated pool (28°C)
  • Poolside loungers & towels
  • Children's pool area

Fine Dining Restaurant

Experience culinary excellence at The Summit — our signature restaurant. Our executive chef crafts seasonal menus using locally sourced mountain ingredients, offering both international cuisine and regional specialties.

  • Breakfast, Lunch & Dinner
  • Rooftop dining available
  • Private dining rooms
  • Curated wine & beverage menu

Mountain View Lounge

Unwind at our cozy lounge with floor-to-ceiling windows framing the spectacular mountain panorama. Enjoy artisan coffees, premium teas, and light bites while soaking in the breathtaking scenery.

  • Open 7 AM – 11 PM
  • Artisan coffee & premium teas
  • Live acoustic music evenings
  • Board games & reading library

Free High-Speed WiFi

Stay connected throughout your stay with complimentary high-speed WiFi available in all rooms, common areas, the pool deck, and the outdoor bar. Perfect for remote workers and digital nomads.

  • Available in all rooms
  • Pool & outdoor coverage
  • Up to 300 Mbps speed
  • No time limits or data caps

Outdoor Bar

Our open-air outdoor bar is the perfect spot to watch the sunset paint the mountains in golden hues. Sip on handcrafted cocktails, local spirits, and refreshing mocktails in a stunning alfresco setting.

  • Open 4 PM – 12 AM daily
  • Handcrafted cocktails & mocktails
  • Live DJ on weekends
  • Mountain sunset views
