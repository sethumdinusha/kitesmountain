DISCOVER BADULLA & ELLA

Iconic Attractions & Adventure Near Kites Mountain

Positioned amidst the misty peaks of Uva Province, Kites Mountain serves as your luxury gateway to explore iconic highland landmarks, ancient historical heritage, and thrilling mountain trails.

Nine Arch Bridge, Demodara

~40 mins drive

Marvel at Sri Lanka’s legendary colonial railway bridge nestled amidst dense tea plantations. Watch the iconic blue train wind through the valley and capture timeless high-country moments.

Bogoda Wooden Bridge & Temple

~20 mins drive

Discover Sri Lanka’s oldest roofed wooden bridge dating back to the 16th century. Tucked beside an ancient temple, it offers a serene journey into authentic island heritage.

Walking Distance / 5 Mins Away

Dunhinda Waterfall

~5 mins away

Located right near Kites Mountain Hotel! Experience Sri Lanka's most iconic, roaring waterfall just a short walk or quick 5-minute ride away.

Ella Rock & Mini Adams Peak

~45 mins drive

Tailor-made for adventure enthusiasts seeking morning hikes with panoramic mountain views. Enjoy breathtaking vistas across the Ella Gap just a short ride away.

OUTDOOR ADVENTURES

🏕️ Hiking & Camping Hotspots Around Badulla

⛰️ Kites Mountain

Walking distance directly from hotel grounds! Enjoy panoramic ridge walks and peaceful nature trails without leaving the property.

👑 Queen's Mountain

Scenic viewpoint & local hiking trail offering spectacular sunrise vistas over the surrounding tea hills and valleys.

⛰️ Narangala Mountain Peak

Famous 360-degree sunrise views & night camping spot known for its distinct pointed peak and golden grass slopes.

🌾 Rathkale Pathana

Peaceful grassy plain & mountain view campsite perfect for day picnics, stargazing, and high-country nature photography.

🌄 Babaragala Pathana

Scenic high-altitude trekking destination surrounded by cool breezes, pine trees, and sweeping mountain panoramas.

🥾 Ella Rock & Mini Adams Peak

Popular trekking trails with iconic mountain gap views, ideal for morning excursions from Kites Mountain Hotel.

COLONIAL & HERITAGE LANDMARKS

🏛️ Historic & Local Attractions

🏛️ Muthiyangana Raja Maha Viharaya

Ancient Historic Temple (~10 mins away). One of Sri Lanka's 16 sacred Solosmasthana places visited by the Buddha.

🚂 Demodara Railway Loop

Engineering Marvel (~25 mins away). A unique spiral railway loop where the train passes underneath its own track station.

🕰️ Badulla Colonial Clock Tower & Market

Historic Town Center (~10 mins away). Explore vibrant local spice stalls, tea shops, and colonial architecture.

🗺️ Need Help Planning Your Hike or Tour?

Staying at Kites Mountain Hotel gives you easy access to many hidden hiking trails, waterfalls, and camping spots around Badulla! Contact our front desk upon arrival—we are happy to guide you, provide expert local advice, and organize custom transport or guides for your adventure!

About Us

A Retreat Above the Clouds

Kites Mountain Hotel & Resort is a premier luxury hotel in Badulla, offering an exquisite alpine escape high in the hills of Sri Lanka's Uva Province. Designed for travelers seeking tranquility and style, our sanctuary features spacious mountain view rooms equipped with private balconies, air-conditioning, and elegant bathrooms.

Whether you are relaxing by our spectacular outdoor swimming pool or enjoying a complimentary daily breakfast, Kites Mountain provides the perfect base for exploring landmarks like Dunhinda Falls, Ella, and the Nine Arch Bridge.
