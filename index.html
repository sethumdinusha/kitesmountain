About Us

A Retreat Above the Clouds

Kites Mountain Hotel & Resort is a comfortable mountain retreat in Badulla, offering family rooms with private bathrooms, air-conditioning, and private balconies. Each room is fitted with a sofa bed, TV, and dining area for a relaxed, homely stay.

Guests enjoy an airport shuttle, an open-air bath, a year-round outdoor swimming pool, and free WiFi throughout the property. A full English/Irish breakfast with juice and fruit is served daily, with room service available for relaxed dining.

  • Daily Breakfast Included
  • Outdoor Swimming Pool
  • Free High-Speed WiFi
